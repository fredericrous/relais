Every finding is fixed except the ones named here, which are **kept** with
their reason:

- **`ci.from-first-commit`** — CI landed at commit 12 of 26. Historical and
  not fixable retroactively; this repository's history is the recorded
  *source* of ADR-0017, which is the decision that exists because of it.
- **P13, second half — `superseded_by` is still never written.** The column
  exists and a revision's classification is complete without it; nothing
  reads it, so writing it would be state with no reader. `changed_controls`
  does now account for `decomposition`, which was the acted-on half.
- **P14 — `RunId`/`DispatchId` still dissolve to `&str` at the ledger's own
  row helpers.** The newtypes hold at every boundary above it; inside the
  module that owns the table, the SQL parameter is a string either way, and
  threading the newtype through `rusqlite`'s parameter traits buys a
  conversion, not a check.
- **`Backend::probe` still answers `Option<Capabilities>`.** The trait is the
  seam a second provider implements, and the probe's failure is now carried
  beside it (`ClaudeBackend::probe_report`, used by `doctor` and by the
  blocked launch), rather than widening every implementor's signature for one
  implementor's diagnostics.
- **17 `_ =>` arms in production.** Every one is over a foreign subject —
  `&str`, an integer, an errno, `io::ErrorKind`, `serde_json::Value`, or a
  tuple/`Option` of those. None is over an enum this crate owns. The two
  that were (`context`'s `AvalVerdict`, `route`'s `Option<Tier>`) are
  exhaustive as of this pull request.
- **25 `let _ =` and 14 `.ok()` in production.** Each is best-effort cleanup
  or a cache, and each carries a one-line comment at the site saying why the
  failure does not change the outcome. The ones that *did* change an outcome
  — the capability probe, the check inventory, the registry and ledger opens
  in `plan`/`doctor`/`coordinator`, the dispatch-intent row — are propagated
  or reported as of this pull request.
- **30 `expect`s in production.** 27 are `serde_json` on owned structs, 3 are
  the stdio pipes `Command` was configured with. All state their invariant in
  the message. There are no `unwrap`, `panic!`, `unreachable!` or `todo!`,
  and no `.lock().expect` anywhere: `FixedClock` recovers a poisoned lock the
  way the coordinator's `lock_state` does.
- **20 `unwrap_or_default()` in production.** Every one is over an `Option`
  that carries no error (a missing JSON field, an absent `PATH`, a result
  text the worker did not produce), not over a `Result`.
- **30 `Instant::now()` in production.** All at a boundary that passes the
  clock inward: the `LocalGate` implementation of `Gate`, the coordinator's
  accept and reconcile loops, the runner's own deadline loops. The pure
  modules are checked by script, below.
